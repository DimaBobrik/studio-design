# Reference cases index (88 anonymized cards)

Study notes on real award / benchmark sites, scanned 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-01 (desktop 1440, mobile 390, home + up to 3 inner pages). The skill was distilled from **~880 scanned sites**: ≈200 award-gallery sites, ≈320 Israeli sites (agencies + their clients), ≈260 client sites built by world-leading agencies and ≈100 agency/studio own sites. Only anonymized study notes are included here: no site names, URLs or per-site rows. Aggregated numbers and "what award sites do that templates don't": `patterns.md`.

Each card in `cases/<id>.md` has: front matter (`descriptor`, region, type, platform, libs, direction match, source), DNA (hero/nav/footer/sections with catalog ids), signature moves, tokens (real fonts + free + Hebrew substitutes, px scale, hexes, radii, paddings), motion, layout, imagery, page set, mobile, steal/don't steal, ACF hints.

Rules: extract structure, never pixels. Copy and assets of the studied sites are never reused in client output. Cards whose `source` or `scan_quality` notes a partial or LOW-quality capture were built partly from extracted data - verify before relying on their layout claims.

Sources (front-matter `source`): `award gallery scan 2026-09` (43 cards; 18 of them home-only from the mass scan) · `world agency client scan 2026-10` (15) · `agency own site scan 2026-10` (17) · `Israeli scan 2026-10` (13, incl. 4 studio own sites).

**World-agency client work (2026-10):** `agency-world.md` (what 41 top studios repeatedly ship for clients, anonymized studio-signature table, WordPress/Shopify-specialist lens, techniques with vanilla recipes, stats vs `patterns.md`) and the 15 cards with `source: world agency client scan`.

**Agency / studio own sites (2026-10):** `agency-sites.md` (97 studio homes + 162 case pages: hero types, work index and case-study anatomy with measures, IL vs world, 20 rules) and the agency own-site cards (case-03, -09, -10, -13, -22, -32, -47, -68, -69, -72, -73, -87; the Israeli studio cards case-11, -31, -53, -77 also apply).

**Hebrew / Israeli briefs:** read `israel.md` (Hebrew type usage + free substitutes, RTL craft, local template look, Israeli e-commerce, skeletons) and the IL cards (`source: Israeli scan 2026-10`).

How to use: after shortlisting a direction (`../directions/_index.md`), open the 1-3 cards whose `direction_match` includes it, and the cards listed under "best for studying" for the page you are building (e.g. PDP -> case-76, case-65, case-56, case-88).
