Which websites must meet Israeli accessibility regulations?

Any business that offers a service to the public online, from a clinic to an online shop, must meet Israeli Standard 5568 at level AA. Small businesses with an annual turnover under ₪300,000 have lighter duties, but not an exemption.

Is an accessibility plugin enough?

No. Overlay widgets change colours and font sizes, but they do not fix missing labels, keyboard traps or images without text alternatives. Courts have treated them as insufficient on their own.

What does a lawsuit usually claim?

Most claims cite a missing accessibility statement, forms that cannot be completed with a keyboard, and low colour contrast. Statutory damages can reach ₪50,000 per claim without proof of harm.

How long does an audit take?
